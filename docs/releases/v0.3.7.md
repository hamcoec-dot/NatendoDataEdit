## What's new in 0.3.7

Find and Replace, part one of a bigger update.

**A count as you type.** The find bar says how many cells match ("1,204 matches"), and "3 of 1,204" once you are on one. Every match on screen is lit up in the grid. The count is worked out in the background and never holds up typing.

**Whole word and ignore accents.** "Ann" can find "Ann Lee" without "Annette", and "Jose" can find "José" (also with the accent typed as a separate mark). Both are off until you tick them.

**Tabs and line breaks.** Tick `\t \n` and `\t`, `\n`, `\r`, `\\` and `\u{A0}` stand for those characters, in Find and in Replace with. Regular expression replacements understand `\n` and `\t` too. In lists, line breaks show as ⏎ and tabs as →.

**Hidden columns are skipped** unless you tick "Include hidden", as in Excel.

**Find all, improved** (Alt+Enter): all listed matches (up to 10,000) scroll smoothly, the arrow keys and Enter move through them, buttons show how many matches each column has and show one column at a time, and you can order them by row or by column.

**Replace all shows every change.** The preview pages through all of them, 50 at a time, with what is taken out (red) and put in (green) marked inside each cell. Click a place to go there. Ctrl+Alt+Enter opens it from the keyboard.

**Group names checked.** A regular expression replacement that names a group the pattern does not have, such as `$2` with one group, or `$1a` (read as a group called "1a"), is caught with a hint (write `${1}a`) before anything changes.

**Keys.** While the find bar has focus: Alt+C match case, Alt+W whole word, Alt+R regular expression, Alt+L selection or whole file. Shift+F4 is another Find next key, as in Excel. Up and Down in the boxes bring back earlier searches; they are kept only until you close the app, and the ? button has Clear search history.

**Right-click a cell:** Find this value, Replace this value, or Find all like this in the column.

**Tips.** The ? button lists wildcard and regular expression examples and the keys.

**Name your tabs.** Tabs made by a tool are now named after what made them instead of "Untitled": for example "Pivot – city by party (voters)", "Rows for one city (voters)", "Subtotals (voters)", "Sample (voters)" or "Compare voters with voters-2024". Any tab that is not saved yet can be renamed: double-click it, right-click › Rename, or press F2 on it. Save then suggests that name for the file, so several pivots from one file are easy to tell apart.

**Layout.** The find bar now has its options on a row of their own under the search.

## Install

1. Download `Natendo.Data.Edit_0.3.7_x64-setup.exe` below.
2. Run it. It installs for your Windows account only and does not need administrator rights. If an older version is installed, close the app first; setup installs over it.

The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC" the first time. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.

This installer was built by GitHub Actions straight from the code in this repository.
